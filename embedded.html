APPROACH & SCOPE

Intelligence running close to the hardware.

Hardware only comes alive when paired with deterministic, resilient firmware. We architect bare-metal and RTOS-based embedded software on STM32, ESP32, nRF, and industrial microcontrollers—prioritizing low latency, fault recovery, and ultra-low power consumption.

Whether your device operates standalone on a coin cell for years or streams telemetry over BLE, Wi-Fi, LoRaWAN, CAN-FD, or Modbus, we build clean, modular codebases ready for over-the-air updates and production scaling.

SPEC_01

RTOS & Bare-Metal Firmware

Deterministic C/C++ development using FreeRTOS, Zephyr, and ESP-IDF with hardware-level debugging.

SPEC_02

Wireless & Industrial Protocols
